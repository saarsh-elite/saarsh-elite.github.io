A safe ride does not end at the gate.

Late one night, I was returning from the airport. Finding a cab was hard. Feeling safe was harder.

When I reached home, my family was asleep. I stood outside our door for a long time, calling again and again. Dogs were barking. Strangers walked past. I was afraid, until someone finally woke up and opened the door.

Later I saw the same fear everywhere: women who stay in the office until morning after a night shift, women dropped at the gate while the cab drives away, and elderly parents whose children live far away and who cannot go out alone.

So I am building SAARSH ÉLITE around one promise: our chauffeur waits until you are safely inside.

“I believe safety should not be a premium feature. It should be the promise.”

Sana Mansoori, Founder, SAARSH ÉLITE
