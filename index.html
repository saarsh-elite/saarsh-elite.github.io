Our story

A safe ride does not end at the gate.

Late one night, I was returning from the airport. Finding a cab was hard. Feeling safe was harder.

When I reached home, my family was asleep. I stood outside our door for a long time, calling again and again. Dogs were barking. Strangers walked past. I was afraid, until someone finally woke up and opened the door.

Later I saw the same fear everywhere: women who stay in the office until morning after a night shift, women dropped at the gate while the cab drives away, and elderly parents whose children live far away.

“Safety should not be a premium feature. It should be the promise.”

Sana Mansoori
Founder, SAARSH ÉLITE

Privacy policy

Your privacy, in plain words.

Last updated: 12 November 2026

1. Who we are

SAARSH ÉLITE is a women-first premium chauffeur service preparing to launch in Bengaluru, India, founded by Sana Mansoori. Our ride service and app are not live yet. The company is being set up; once it is registered, this policy will name the legal entity.

2. What we collect today

This website has no sign-up, no accounts, no advertising and no analytics or tracking cookies. It remembers only your sound on/off choice, in your own browser. The "Plan a ride" box does not send anything to us: it only opens your own email app with your trip details, and nothing reaches us unless you press send. We only receive what you choose to send us by email or LinkedIn message:

  • Riders requesting early access: the trip details and contact details in your email.
  • Companies and fleet partners: your name, company, work email or phone, and your message.
  • Chauffeurs registering interest: your name, phone number, driving experience and anything else you choose to share.

Please do not send us ID documents, licence copies or bank details at this stage. We will ask for them only when hiring opens, and tell you why.

3. How we use it

  • To reply to you.
  • To contact you when rides open, or to consider you for a job, partnership or client contract.

We never sell your information. We never charge any fee for jobs or training.

4. Services this website uses

The website is hosted on GitHub Pages, loads fonts from Google Fonts and photos from Unsplash. Like any website, these services receive your device's IP address when the page loads, under their own privacy policies. Our email runs on Gmail (Google).

5. Sharing

We do not share your information with anyone, except the service providers above and where the law requires it.

6. How long we keep it

We keep messages and requests for up to 12 months, or until you ask us to delete them, whichever comes first.

7. Your rights

Under India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023, you can ask us to show, correct or delete your information, or withdraw your consent, at any time. Write to saarsh.elite@gmail.com. We will reply within 7 days and resolve your request within 30 days. If you are not satisfied, you may complain to the Data Protection Board of India.

8. Children

This website is not meant for anyone under 18. Please do not send us a child's information.

9. When our app launches

Rides will need more information, such as pickup and drop locations, live trip location, emergency contacts and payments. Before any ride service starts, we will publish an updated policy and ask for your consent in the app.
